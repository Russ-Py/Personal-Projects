## HBT and GRA Experiments
Contained in the HBT_and_GRA_to_Assert_Quantum_Nature_of_Light file is a technical report I wrote outlining my recreation of the Hanbury Brown-Twiss (HBT) and Grangier, Rogers, Aspect (GRA) experiments. I was responsible for positioning optical equioment and aligning an optical laser to show that light is comprised of discrete energy quanta (photons).
